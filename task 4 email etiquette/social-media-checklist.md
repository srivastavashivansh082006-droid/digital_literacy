# Social Media Do's and Don'ts Checklist
### For College Students — Digital Ambassador Program (Module 4)

---

## ✔ DO's

1. **Think before you post** — Ask yourself: would a future employer or professor be comfortable seeing this? If in doubt, don't post.
2. **Keep your profile professional** — Use a clear, appropriate photo and an accurate bio. Your digital profile is often your first impression.
3. **Use privacy settings** — Review and apply privacy controls on each platform so that only the right audience sees your content.
4. **Give credit to original creators** — When sharing photos, quotes, or ideas that are not your own, always attribute the source.
5. **Engage respectfully in debates** — You can disagree online. Do so with evidence and civility, not insults or personal attacks.
6. **Fact-check before sharing** — Verify information from credible sources before amplifying it. Sharing misinformation reflects poorly on you.
7. **Log out of shared or public devices** — Always sign out after using library computers or shared phones to protect your accounts.

---

## ✘ DON'Ts

1. **Don't share personal information publicly** — Avoid posting your phone number, home address, or daily routine on public-facing profiles.
2. **Don't engage in or spread hate speech or cyberbullying** — Online harassment has serious personal, academic, and legal consequences.
3. **Don't vent about professors, classmates, or workplaces online** — Even indirect posts can be traced back and damage important relationships.
4. **Don't post during classes, exams, or professional events** — It signals disrespect and poor digital discipline.
5. **Don't overshare** — Not every thought or personal event needs to be documented. Maintain healthy boundaries between your online and offline life.
6. **Don't accept requests from unknown individuals** — Be cautious about who can access your profile and content online.
7. **Don't assume anything is truly private** — Screenshots exist. Even "private" stories and messages can be saved and shared without your consent.
